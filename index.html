About

Chrona is an ambient display platform for institutional spaces where time is not just shown — it is felt.

Most digital signage is designed to fill a screen. Chrona is designed to belong to a room. The clock is large because presence matters. The background moves slowly because stillness without motion becomes hollow. Every typographic and motion decision is restrained by intention.

Built for projectors, LED walls, and auditorium screens. Designed to work gracefully with or without internet. Tested for examination halls, assemblies, lobbies, and events.
