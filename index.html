About
Chrona

There are spaces where time carries weight — where what the clock says matters more than what anyone in the room does. Chrona exists for those spaces. Not to decorate a screen, but to hold a room's attention without demanding it.

Most display software is built around features. Chrona was built around a feeling — that a well-designed ambient display should feel inevitable, like it always belonged there. Calm. Precise. Present. Every decision from typography to motion was made in service of that feeling, and nothing else.

Built for projectors, LED walls, and large screens in institutional environments. Designed to work gracefully with or without internet. Tested across examination sessions, assemblies, and formal events. Configurable without a manual.
